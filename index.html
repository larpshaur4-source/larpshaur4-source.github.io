Toon Shorts Studio

Toon Shorts Studio is a desktop app that turns an idea into a short animated video, with voice and music, and posts it to your own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

How it works

  • Type an idea. The app writes a short script and animates it as a cartoon.
  • Connect your TikTok account with TikTok's own login.
  • Before anything is posted, you see a preview, pick who can see it, choose comment, Duet and Stitch settings, and disclose branded content if needed.
  • Nothing is posted until you press Post.
