Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

  • When you connect TikTok, the app receives a login token and your basic profile (name and avatar). It uses them only to show who is posting and to post the videos you choose.
  • The token is stored only on your computer. It is never sold, shared or sent anywhere except to TikTok when posting.
  • The app collects no data from other TikTok users.
  • To remove access, disconnect the app in TikTok's settings under Security, then Manage app permissions.
